Lasagna

There is nothing better than a warm, comforting dish of homemade lasagna. Layered with rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and perfectly cooked pasta, this classic Italian recipe is the ultimate comfort food for Sunday family dinners.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan Cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook the beef in a pan until it turns brown.
  2. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  3. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 30 minutes until the cheese melts.
  4. Serving and ENJOY!
